# Two-economy model: results

Generated by `two_economy.py`. Illustrative, not a forecast; see MODEL.md for assumptions.


## Scenarios

- **Comparator: the loom** — Capability plateaus well short of a general substitute and most new tasks go to humans. This is what the standard reassurance (steam, combustion, transistor) assumes.
- **Managed dependency** — Nationalist-oligarchic consolidation with a dole. Transfers expand quickly enough to close most of the consumption hole; rents stay concentrated; asset values rise.
- **Durable fragmentation** — Unresolved at 2040. Adoption is slower and patchier (state-level friction), transfers are partial and capped by politics, a chronic demand shortfall persists without acute collapse.
- **Compensated restructuring** — The British buyout. Transfers lag, a crisis arrives, and the response is public equity in the AI value chain paid out as dividends, with private holders partly compensated.
- **Capability diffusion** — Open weights and cheap silicon flatten the gradient. Most AI gains are competed into lower prices rather than captured as rents; more new tasks are run by humans with cheap AI.
- **Prolonged disorder** — Crisis without reconstruction. Transfers lag, the demand hole opens, the shock overwhelms institutional capacity, and nothing coherent replaces it; Social Security takes its scheduled cut.
