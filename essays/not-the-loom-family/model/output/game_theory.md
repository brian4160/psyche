## 2. Four small games


#### The capability race (lab vs. lab; US vs. China)

| Lab A \ Lab B | Restrain | Race |
|---|---|---|
| **Restrain** | 7, 7 | 1, 9 |
| **Race** | 9, 1 | 4, 4 |

Pure-strategy Nash equilibria: (Race, Race).

A prisoner's dilemma. Mutual restraint is better for both than mutual racing, but each does better racing whatever the other does. Without an enforceable agreement the equilibrium is (Race, Race). This is why capability concentration keeps rising and why no lab's stated values are a reliable guide to its behavior: the game, not the values, sets the behavior.

#### Federal preemption of state AI law (the free-soil fight)

| Federal executive \ States (CA, MA) | Resist | Comply |
|---|---|---|
| **Preempt** | 6, 4 | 9, 2 |
| **Defer** | 3, 8 | 5, 5 |

Pure-strategy Nash equilibria: (Preempt, Resist).

The executive prefers to preempt whatever the states do; the states prefer to resist whatever the executive does. Equilibrium (Preempt, Resist) is a standoff that the courts and Congress resolve over years. That is durable fragmentation as a transient, exactly as the essay describes, and it is why the essay lists preemption as the single most informative signal.

#### Open versus closed weights (frontier leader vs. laggard)

| Leader \ Laggard | Close | Open |
|---|---|---|
| **Close** | 9, 3 | 7, 6 |
| **Open** | 5, 2 | 4, 5 |

Pure-strategy Nash equilibria: (Close, Open).

The leader always prefers to keep its frontier closed; the laggard prefers to open (commoditize the leader's advantage, win developers, set standards). Equilibrium (Close, Open) produces exactly the world we see: a closed frontier and an open commodity tier a step behind. Capability diffusion needs the gap between them to shrink, which depends on compute access and on whether the frontier plateaus.

#### The adopter's dilemma (the consumption contradiction)

| Firm A \ All other firms | Keep workers | Substitute |
|---|---|---|
| **Keep workers** | 6, 6 | 2, 8 |
| **Substitute** | 8, 2 | 3, 3 |

Pure-strategy Nash equilibria: (Substitute, Substitute).

Each firm is better off substituting whatever others do; if all substitute, aggregate demand falls and all are worse off than if none had. No firm can fix this alone (Henry Ford's wage worked because he was large relative to his own market; nobody is now). The collective fix is a transfer system funded by all: this is why the capital class itself ends up supporting a dole, and why the dole is the stable form of consolidation.

Reading the table. With a weak coalition, capital's best move is a small dole and the outcome is managed dependency with near certainty. As crisis deepens and cohesion rises, capital raises the dole to buy off mobilization: the dole is the instrument by which the capital class steers the system away from a buyout, and it is cheap relative to what a buyout would cost. A buyout becomes a live outcome only when the coalition is cohesive enough that even a large dole leaves mobilization probable. This is the British case: a credible movement plus a capital class that preferred sale to a fight. The American case is what happens when the sale is refused.

When mobilization is likely to succeed, capital pays more to prevent it. This is why the information environment and the identity split matter so much to the capital class: they lower q and cohesion, which lowers the price of stability.

## 4. Household positioning under uncertainty


Scores are 0–10 judgments of how well a household following each strategy fares in each terminal state. 'Regret' is the gap to the best available strategy in that state; minimax regret picks the strategy whose worst-case regret is smallest.

| Labor-only, exposed cognitive work, no buffer | 3 | 4 | 6 | 5 | 1 | 3 | 3.7 | 1 | 6 |
| Labor-only, presence or licensed work | 5 | 6 | 7 | 6 | 2 | 4 | 5.2 | 2 | 4 |
| Cognitive work + deploy the tools + cash buffer | 5 | 6 | 7 | 7 | 3 | 4 | 5.5 | 3 | 4 |
| Wages + some broad capital exposure + buffer | 7 | 6 | 6 | 6 | 3 | 4 | 6.0 | 3 | 2 |
| Concentrated bet on AI leaders | 9 | 5 | 4 | 2 | 1 | 2 | 5.6 | 1 | 5 |
| Concentrated bet against AI (cash, no equities) | 2 | 4 | 4 | 4 | 5 | 4 | 3.3 | 2 | 7 |

Minimax-regret strategies (max regret 2): **Wages + some broad capital exposure + buffer**. Highest expected score: **Wages + some broad capital exposure + buffer** (6.0).

The point is not the exact scores. It is the shape: strategies that spread a household across the two economies (some wage income that is hard to substitute, some ownership of the capital side, and a buffer) are never the best in any single state and never the worst in any, which is what you want when you cannot know which state arrives. Concentrated bets in either direction are the best in one state and the worst in others.
